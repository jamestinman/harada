Description
Find the words, solve the murder in this all-action puzzle game set in 1980s small town America.

"Who is that man? Why can't I remember anything?!"
Piece together clues to help Laura find the identity of the true culprit and clear her own name.

Search for words in any direction, building up a chain to complete the level and earn power-ups, solving the mystery one letter at a time.

Description (gemini)
1980s Small Town America. A body on the floor. A missing memory.

You wake up on the floor of a diner. Your head is pounding. There is a gun in your hand and a dead body on the checkerboard lino. The police are on their way... and you can’t remember a thing.

Welcome to WorderMurder.

Step into Laura's work shoes, a waitress caught in a nightmare, as she races to piece together her fragmented memory before she gets caught for a crime she didn't commit.

UNRAVEL THE MYSTERY Dive into a gripping noir thriller where every puzzle you solve unlocks a new piece of the story. Who is the victim? Why were you there? And who is the real killer watching from the shadows?

CONNECT THE CLUES This isn’t just a word search; it’s an investigation.

Swipe to connect letters, like a wordsearch but in any direction, chaining words to clear levels.

Solve puzzles to find clues and earn coins and power-ups.

FEATURES:

Deep Narrative: A twisty murder mystery story inspired by classic 1980s crime thrillers.

Brain-Training Gameplay: Hundreds of levels that start easy but will challenge even the best word sleuths.

Strategic Power-Ups: Stuck on a suspect? Use boosters to offers hints, blow out obstacles or reveal the hidden words.

Stunning Comic Art: Immerse yourself in a beautifully illustrated graphic novel style.
